# Registries

- `states.csv` - the 50 contest states plus DC (Census FIPS, region, 2025 population).
- `entities.csv` - 2,558 professional franchises and college programs (institution x sport x sex) in 18 sport families; schema in `docs/DATA_DICTIONARY.md`.
- `sports.csv` - sport and competition families with their screening status.
- `rivalry_edges.csv` - 1,596 directed rivalry edges (analyst tiers; generated by `scripts/build_H_M.py` from `config/rivalry_curated.yaml`).
