# Independent check: do Wikipedia readers see the same rivalry ladder?

Auto-generated by `scripts/wikipedia_rivalry.py`. No Google data. For curated pro rivalries with a Wikipedia article: article page views (2021 to 2025) divided by the geometric mean of the two teams' own page views (removes team popularity), regressed on the analyst tier with a league effect. exp(coefficient) is the readers' ladder: tier-2 and tier-3 interest relative to tier 1.

Rivalries with an article: **68** (tier 1: 14, tier 2: 27, tier 3: 27). Rank correlation of reading interest with tier: **0.49**.

| | Wikipedia readers (90% interval) | Analyst prior | Google antipathy fit (NFL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 2 relative to tier 1 | 0.36 (0.24 to 0.53) | 0.38 | 0.41 |
| Tier 3 relative to tier 1 | 0.27 (0.18 to 0.40) | 0.11 | 0.11 |

Caveats: national reading, no state detail; reading is not hostility; only rivalries that have an article are included (a selection toward famous ones); the tier of a pair is the stronger of its two directed tiers. Pairs and article titles: `data/model/wikipedia_rivalry.csv`.
