Reading: a positive within-state Spearman means that, in a typical state, the teams people most often call out as bad are the teams the model says that state is most hostile to. The pooled and within-team columns remove each team's national level (a team hated everywhere, like the Cowboys, is not a test of local rivalry) and ask whether the *geography* of antipathy matches the model's. Antipathy searches also capture non-rivalry dislike (winning, arrogance, fan behavior), so perfect agreement is not expected; a clearly positive value supports the structure, a near-zero value is a finding about the model.
