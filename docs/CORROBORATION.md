# Corroboration and type of the leading pairs

Generated by `scripts/corroboration.py`. For each leading pair I ask whether evidence that does not depend on my team-level model points the same way. I fixed the three thresholds before computing this table, but after I had already seen the state-level ranks of the lead pairs (a disclosed limitation, see `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`).

- **C1, state-level framing:** people search against the state itself. The pair ranks within the top 5% of the 677 pairs measured in both directions (rank 33 or better).
- **C2, searches-only score:** a pair score built only from antipathy searches (no tiers, no attention weights) ranks the pair within the top 25% of the 73 pairs where it can be computed. Pairs that cannot be scored are inconclusive, not failures.
- **C3, reciprocity:** the smaller of the two directed hostilities is at least 40% of the larger (ratio 0.4 or more).
- **Type:** *marquee-matchup* if the effective number of channels (N_eff) is below 1.5 in either direction, otherwise *system*.

These conditions describe a result. They never change a score.
